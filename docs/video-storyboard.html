How to read this

  • This is a sketch with simple CSS moves, not the final film. The real one will have smoother camera moves, real Lio screens inside Apple device frames, and music.
  • Order follows our priorities: categories (Problem 3), then prices (Problem 2), then PDF quotes (Problem 1), then auto-approval (Problem 4) as the bonus, in black like the bonus card on the case study.
  • Diagrams: solid box is code, dashed box is the AI, a teal pill is a yes or no check, black is where it ends with a person. Checks are pills, not diamonds, to keep to the no-diagonal rule.
  • Each problem follows the same arc: what goes wrong, the options side by side with their cost, the chosen one and why, a diagram of how it now works, then the numbers.
  • real marks numbers from our evals today. after marks a number we fill in once the fix is built and the eval is run again. Nothing is made up.
